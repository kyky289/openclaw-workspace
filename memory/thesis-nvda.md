# Thesis Record — NVIDIA (NVDA)

## 2026-09-15 (initial)

**Price context:** $212.17 close 2026-09-15, mkt cap $5.12T, TTM P/E 26.8, fwd P/E 17.6, P/FCF 40.3.
52wk price +19.3% vs TTM EPS +~120% → heavy multiple compression. 200DMA 197.79, 50DMA 213.24, RSI 45.

**Current thesis (medium confidence):** The debate is no longer "is AI demand real" — Q2 FY27 (ended 2026-07-26) settles that for the next ~4 quarters. The debate is (a) whether the FY28 +70% guide is *collectible* given who is paying, and (b) terminal gross margin. At ~13-14x a FY28 that management itself guided, the market is explicitly refusing to underwrite that guide.

**Key facts (primary sources: Q2 FY27 press release, 10-Q filed 2026-08, CFO commentary):**
- Q2 FY27 rev $96.221B (+106% YoY, +18% QoQ); DC $89.0B (+117% YoY, 92.5% of total); GAAP GM 75.0%; GAAP dil EPS $2.46, non-GAAP $2.22.
- Q3 FY27 guide $108B ±2%, GM 74.0%, **assumes zero China DC compute revenue**.
- GM guided to trough 71-72% in Q4 FY27, then 72-73% (memory/HBM/wafer cost). Nvidia raising AI server prices >15% for early-2027 shipments.
- FY28 preliminary guide **+70% YoY** (consensus was ~44%); Kress: "supply-constrained"; customer forecasts "point to our growth doubling next year."
- Commitments table (10-Q Note 10): total **$366B** — Supply & capacity $279B (up from $119B prior quarter), cloud svc $29B, DC leases not commenced $25B, equity investments $25B, capex $8B. Plus additional commitments $56B (AI cloud agreements $36B + 3rd-party DC leases $20B). Plus guarantees **$108.5B** ($3.5B land/power/shell + **$105B SB Energy guarantee for OpenAI**, 4.25GW Pike County OH, first phase FY2029). Grand total >$530B.
- Concentration: Q2 FY27 one direct customer = 16% of revenue (H1: 16/15/13%). AR: five direct customers = 22/14/13/11/10% (= 70% of AR). "One AI research and deployment company" (OpenAI) contributed meaningfully via indirect cloud purchases.
- Balance sheet: AR $63.1B (DSO ~60d, up from ~53d), inventory $31.6B (raw materials $3.8B→$11.3B), non-marketable securities $22.3B→$51.2B, marketable equity $12.9B→$42.8B, LT debt $7.5B→$32.4B.
- Earnings quality flag: H1 other income, net $24.1B (~17% of pretax) largely equity marks on investees who are also customers. Non-GAAP NI $54.0B < GAAP $59.7B.
- Disclosed new practice: 90-day to 1-year payment terms for investment-grade customers on large DC builds.
- Geographic: US $60.1B, Taiwan $27.0B (ODMs), China incl HK $7.88B, Other $1.3B.

**Strongest supporting evidence:** $279B supply commitments (management putting its own balance sheet behind the ramp); DC +117% YoY with broadening beyond hyperscalers (ACIE: enterprise, sovereign, NeoCloud); hyperscaler capex consensus $1.0-1.45T in 2027 (UBS/GS/MS ~+57% YoY); FCF $127B TTM; net cash; buybacks $26B returned in Q2.

**Strongest contrary evidence:**
- Nvidia bonds trade at **BBB-like spreads despite AA ratings** (Morgan Stanley credit, via IFR) — the credit market is pricing circularity risk the equity market is not.
- Custom ASIC unit growth ~45% in 2026 vs ~16% for GPU AI servers (TrendForce); Meta custom chip into DCs H1 2027; Google-Marvell deal; Broadcom ~60% of custom ASIC by 2027 (Counterpoint).
- Gross margin structurally down 75% → 71-73%; memory is the binding constraint and Nvidia does not control it.
- OpenAI: ~$588B cloud commitments vs ~$25B ARR and ~$27B annual burn (reported). Nvidia's $105B SB Energy guarantee sits directly on that credit.
- AI complex gross debt ~$470B; ~$320B issued by 5 hyperscalers + Nvidia in 2026 YTD (JPM).
- Sept 2026 narrative risk: Amodei/frontier-lab calls for slower development; NVDA -3.36% on 2026-09-14.

**Key assumptions:** (1) hyperscaler capex is funded by hyperscaler cash flow, not lab credit, for the FY28 portion; (2) memory cost pass-through holds at >15% price increases without demand destruction; (3) ASICs expand the pie faster than they displace merchant GPU dollars.

**Important unknowns:** true end-demand ROI at the token layer; whether the $105B guarantee phases ever become effective (first phase FY2029); FY28 revenue quality (how much routed through Nvidia-financed entities); China policy path (Huang attending Trump-Xi state dinner ~week of 2026-09-21).

**Confidence: medium.** Near-term (2-3 quarters) high; FY29+ low.

**Invalidation conditions:**
- Q3 FY27 (late Nov 2026) revenue below ~$105B, or FY28 +70% guide cut.
- Q4 FY27 gross margin below 71%, or FY28 GM guided under 72% → cost pass-through failing.
- DSO above ~75 days, or a jump in AR allowances/write-offs → customers can't pay.
- Supply commitments falling QoQ from $279B → management's own demand signal reversing.
- A funding failure at OpenAI/Anthropic/a major neocloud, or Nvidia guarantees moving from disclosure to accrual.
- A hyperscaler explicitly guiding 2027 capex *down*, or disclosing a majority-ASIC accelerator mix.

**Monitor:** Q3 FY27 print (late Nov 2026); hyperscaler Q3/Q4 CY2026 capex guides; NVDA bond spreads vs AA index; HBM pricing (SK hynix/Micron/Samsung); OpenAI/Anthropic funding rounds; BIS/China export policy.

## 2026-09-22 — Update

**Previous thesis:**
(2026-09-21, medium confidence, UNCHANGED) Near-term demand settled; open questions are FY28 collectability (now including deployability: permits, power, land) and terminal gross margin. Added assumption (4): contracted compute can be physically deployed on the schedule FY28 revenue requires.

**New evidence:**
- Texas Gov. Abbott directed TCEQ to halt **all** permits sought by data center projects until the ERCOT/TWDB audits are complete; "no state agency shall move forward with regulatory approvals related to data center development" until the audit data is acquired. No end date. TCEQ compliance update due 2026-10-19. Abbott will seek to eliminate data center financial incentives next legislative session — gov.texas.gov press release (primary), 2026-09-21; Texas Tribune and CNBC, 2026-09-21.
- Texas Tribune: the order appears aimed at behind-the-meter builds (own generation), which had bypassed the August ERCOT grid-connection pause. Non-ERCOT parts of the state are now also covered via TCEQ/PUC/TWDB jurisdiction.
- BloombergNEF (via CNBC; report not inspected): the August Texas pause put almost 20% of the 253 GW US data center pipeline (~50 GW) at risk. Data Center Watch (advocacy group, via CNBC): $68B of development delayed or cancelled in Q2 2026 due to local pushback.
- Local moratoria keep spreading (Marathon County WI 12-month; Jasper County SC first reading; Commerce City CO and Oakland CA considering) — local press, 2026-09-21.
- Not yet corroborated: no hyperscaler, neocloud, or Nvidia statement citing Texas-related deployment delays found.

**Impact on assumptions:**
- Assumption (4) (deployability): weakened. The August pause covered grid interconnection only, and behind-the-meter builds could route around it. The new order closes that route and covers all state approvals in the state that was on track to become the largest US data center market. It is also open-ended, so the "multi-quarter freeze" path is now plausible rather than hypothetical.
- Assumptions (1)-(3): no new evidence.

**Thesis status:**
WEAKENED (at the margin, via assumption 4 only)

**Reasoning:**
Texas is a large share of new capacity (Stargate Abilene; BNEF ~50 GW at risk). An open-ended halt on all state approvals for new projects would mainly hit 2027-2028 energization, which is the FY28-FY29 window that the +70% FY28 guide depends on. The effect is on timing, not demand: already-permitted and already-built sites are not affected, and capacity can move to other states, though NY, OR and many counties are also restricting. Invalidation condition (Texas multi-quarter freeze *cited by hyperscalers or Nvidia as delaying deployments*) is now approached: the first half is plausibly in motion; the second half (a customer or Nvidia citing it) has not occurred. The trigger is election-driven (Abbott reelection 2026-11-03), so reversal after the midterms is possible. Confidence is not lowered, because no order, guidance, or deployment data has changed yet.

**Current thesis:**
Near-term (2-3 quarters) demand settled. FY28 collectability now carries a more concrete deployment-timing risk: US permitting friction is escalating from pauses toward open-ended freezes in the largest growth state. This is more likely to shift revenue from FY28 into FY29 than to remove it, but it raises the chance that the +70% guide slips.

**Confidence:**
Medium — near-term high; FY29+ low. Unchanged in level. Direction of risk to FY28 is now clearly negative on deployability.

**Contrary evidence:**
Open-ended Texas halt on all state data center approvals; spreading state/local moratoria; OpenAI's projected -$278B FCF against Nvidia's $105B guarantee. Supporting: Huang expects ~2x chip unit sales next year; GS sees US hyperscaler capex at $1.4T in 2027; Micron HBM reportedly sold out through 2027 (secondary).

**Invalidation conditions:**
All prior conditions still apply. Sharpened:
